# Session Start

### Day 011

Midday

Last day of Low Fog. Tomorrow will be High Fog.

We pick up having just crossed over to [[Isle Garganta]]. We meet [[Dr Dolus Remidium]], who joins us.

We decide to head towards [[Garganta]], crossing rocky terrain as night falls. We see a small village built into a terrace on a hill. Rough stone wall 10' high surrounds village. A man stands guard, wearing thick leather apron, holding a pike. "State your business, travellers"

We tell him we travelled from [[Isle Norte]] and are seeking shelter. He introduces himself as [[Durreo]]. He asks if we have weapons and says it's odd to see 4 oddly dressed outlanders. He says we need to check our weapons at the gate. He takes the weapons from [[Jase Walker]] and [[Thaddeus Bautista]], but [[Dr Dolus Remidium]] hides his.

We enter the gate and are in a small gated-in area. The guards check our bags, then [[Durreo]] gives us directions to an inn - a place run by [[Aroone]].

Not many people live here. Most are burly / rough, wearing dirty heavy leather smocks (they're minors). Houses are rough contracted stone huts. 

One building is a bit nicer and has columns.

We walk to the inn. It's 2 stories - above the rest of the surrounding 1 story buildings. We enter and the room goes completely silent as all the patrons turn to stare at us. A middle-aged woman stands behind the bar. We ask where we can rent a room and [[Aroone]] beckons us over. As we walk up to the bar, the room empties out. Everyone leaves at once, shooting us dirty looks.

[[Aroone]] seems suspicious of us too. We give her a bushel of ficus leaves to pay for room and food and says she'll give us a modest supper and we can stay in a dorm upstairs. [[Dr Dolus Remidium]] thinks [[Aroone]] is overcharging us. He gets her to give us a nicer meal.

She gives us ale and food. Only one patron remains in the tavern. [[Hector Castle]] goes over and talks to him. He introduces himself as [[Torren]]. He's wearing an oiled leather jacket and pants, not the apron the others are wearing.

"The people here view outsiders as 'unclean'. Some view the rains of this planet as being cleansing of the entire person or soul. Outsiders are seen as unclean and loaded up with the detritus of easy living. I'm what they call a way-finder."

Hector asks what Torren knows about this island and the surrounding isles. Torren smokes an odd-looking black cigarette. He says [[Isle Norte]] is a backwater of a backwater and he never goes there. He refers to the [[Gene Stitchers]] as "witches" and says he doesn't want to become "gristle for their vats".

He says he belongs to a wayfarer's meter that operates independently.

Hector asks if [[House Marina]] rules the entire area. He says they rule [[Isle Norte]] and [[Isle Garganta]] and manage the meters, collect taxes, etc. 

He glances around nervously and says he'd be happy to give us his opinion on politics out on the trail, but there are ears everywhere. "[[Lady Isabella]] does a fine job managing these islands"

[[Aroone]] joins us at our table. "Let me be frank. I want off this planet. I could be of some assistance to you if you can help me with this matter." #quest 

[[Dr Dolus Remidium]] looks him over and notices that the man seems desperate in his ask to get off the planet. He must be hiding something. #clue 

Aroon comes out with [[Meyerback]] meat, fried root vegetables, flatbread, etc. [[Torren]] immediately fixes himself a huge portion of food and says "So I take you to [[Tupak]] and you get me off this rock. What say you?"

We say we need to sleep on it. "I don't think you understand. You _need_ me. Do you have any idea how dangerous the road is? Isle Norte is child's play. The road from here to Tupak is beset by predators - human and non-human alike." We say goodnight and he stuffs some of the food into his pockets before he heads upstairs.

[[Dr Dolus Remidium]] stops him and asks if he'd like to join him for a drink. "Only if you have the sense to let me lead you to Tupak." [[Dr Dolus Remidium]] tries to get him drunk so he can question him more. 

[[Torren]] mentions he is unmetered and being chased by his old gang - [[The Mudlarks]]. #clue 
He says he can get us to [[Tupak]] before they catch up. 

We all head to bed.

### Day 012

[[Aroone]] wakes us up early and tell us it's time to go.

We head out onto the street as she shoos us out.

Tomorrow will be **DELUGE**.

We can't get caught out in deluge, so we find [[Torren]] and say we'll need his help as wayfinder.

He says we'll need to leave at once. We approach the gate and [[Durreo]] gives us back our weapons. As soon as we're outside the gate, [[Torren]] says if we pass through the [[Overgrown Mesa]], we can be in the [[House Marina Estate]] by nightfall. They have a dirigible, but may allow us on it for a price. For them, a trip to the capital is nothing. He says outsiders with weapons are useful to them, but it won't be easy.

Otherwise, we can head to the [[Old Aquaduct]] via [[Fronton]] and continue south. But this route would take weeks longer, but would require us to not have to do favors for the nobles.

We head towards [[Overgrown Mesa]]. A huge green hill covered in scrub and small trees and bushes. We're traveling on an overgrown path and [[Torren]] stops and holds his hand up to get us to stop. As we do, we hear the leaves moving in the trees and he yells "run!" #encounter

We run into a path and see a dilapidated tower - the upper half has been sheared off and the stone blocks are scattered. [[Thaddeus Bautista]] and [[Jase Walker]] stumble on roots of a tree and fall.

The creature claws [[Thaddeus Bautista]].

We barely make it into a ruined structure at the base of the broken tower. [[Torren]] says the creature was a [[Canopy Crawler]] and it'll chase us when we leave too.
