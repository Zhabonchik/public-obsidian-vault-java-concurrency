## Description

Desert, but torrential rain every few days. Seas form, but boil off within a few days and become thick clouds. Cycle repeats.

Side effect of weather: cannot develop higher tech

Radios, computers, etc do not work
Dangerous to take off and land from planet

Contains [[Scout Service]] embassy in [[Tupak]]

Spires around the planet are visual markers used for navigation (due to having no radio / long range communication) and points of interest. Trade done by dirigible, so they navigate to the spires to set up. Also used for semaphore signals.

Water cycles:
Each part of cycle lasts 1d6 days. Change is obvious with 1 day warning.

- Deluge - torrential rain, must shelter
- Placid - seas have formed, only high ground available
- Low Fog - Oceans significantly boiled off, lowland swampy
- High Fog - Oceans completely dry
